pyspi/statistics/infotheory.py implements the Kraskov-Stoegbauer-Grassberger
(KSG), box-kernel, Gaussian and symbolic estimators of mutual information and
transfer entropy, and the Kozachenko-Leonenko entropy estimator, following --
and validated against -- the Java Information Dynamics Toolkit (JIDT),
https://github.com/jlizier/jidt, which pyspi 2.x called through JPype. The
estimator definitions, parameter names (k_HISTORY, DYN_CORR_EXCL, KERNEL_WIDTH,
AUTO_EMBED_METHOD, ...) and default behaviours derive from JIDT.

The JIDT jar is no longer distributed with pyspi. Its notice is reproduced here.
